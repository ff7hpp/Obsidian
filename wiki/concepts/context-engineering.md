# Context Engineering

Context engineering is the practice of packaging the right rules, source files, specs, decisions, errors, and summaries so an agent can work accurately without repeated explanation.

## What To Store

- Project rules and `CODEX.md` instructions.
- Specs, architecture notes, and ADRs.
- Relevant source files and repo maps.
- Error outputs and reproduction steps.
- Design handoffs and product requirements.
- Source summaries in `wiki/sources/`.
- Cross-links to entities, concepts, and projects.

## For This Vault

This vault is itself a context-engineering system. `raw/` stores original inputs, `wiki/` stores distilled agent-usable knowledge, `wiki/index.md` is the retrieval map, and `wiki/log.md` is the timeline.
