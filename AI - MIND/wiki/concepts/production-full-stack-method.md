# Production Full-Stack Method

Reusable Codex method for auditing and building websites across the full delivery stack.

## Layers

Product scope, UX/design, frontend, backend/API, database/storage, authentication/authorization, security, testing/QA, performance/reliability, deployment/CI-CD, observability/operations, and evidence-based release verification.

## Operating Rule

Separate verified current state from target requirements. Work in vertical slices, keep secrets out of reports, test authorization negatively, and do not call a product production-ready without build/runtime/security/launch evidence.

## Reuse

Use the source skill and checklist in `raw/production-full-stack-skill/`, and adapt the website blueprint in [[projects/geoissue-full-stack-website]] to the project's domain.
